Prototype for schoolcompass.uk — what schools publish about curriculum, clubs, ethos and related topics. Summaries are drawn from scanned public pages; expand any section to see links and quoted excerpts.

Prototype · Hampshire · scanned — Legacy score-based viewer
Not a school rating. This shows what was found (or not found) on school websites and linked documents during an automated scan. It does not judge quality or compare schools on an absolute scale. Always visit schools and read original sources before deciding.

What this pilot is learning to look for

As schools are scanned, recurring URL and page-title terms are collected to improve discovery on later sites — similar themes may surface as new focus areas over time.
